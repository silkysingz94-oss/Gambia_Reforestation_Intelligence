Methodology
NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red)
EVI = 2.5 × (NIR − Red) / (NIR + 6Red − 7.5Blue + 1)
SAVI = 1.5 × (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red + 0.5)

Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance is used. Vegetation classes are screening categories based on NDVI thresholds, not tree-instance detection.

Interpretation and limitations
NDVI-based classes indicate spectral vegetation conditions. They do not distinguish planted trees from natural vegetation, shrubs or crops. Area is an approximate estimate based on valid output-grid pixels. Tree counts and survival rates require validated field observations or a trained segmentation model.
