04 / METHODOLOGY AND EXPORTS

Processing workflow

1. Search Sentinel-2 L2A imagery intersecting the study site.
2. Retrieve signed public cloud-optimised GeoTIFF assets.
3. Read red, green, blue, NIR and scene-classification bands.
4. Mask cloud and shadow classes.
5. Calculate NDVI, EVI and SAVI indicators.
6. Calculate vegetation-pixel proportions and compare observations over time.
7. Optionally screen local drone imagery or load a compatible ONNX model.

Scientific validation

Vegetation increases may reflect natural regeneration, seasonal grasses, crops or planted trees. Reliable tree-survival assessment requires repeat field observations, georeferenced planting locations and independently validated image labels. A U-Net++ model must be trained and evaluated using representative Gambian drone or satellite imagery.

*Vegetated area is an approximate estimate based on the sampled raster window and nominal 10 m pixels. The first implementation uses the AOI bounding rectangle for raster sampling; a drawn polygon's exact interior is not yet used to mask pixels. Satellite imagery is accessed online; uploaded drone images are processed locally.
